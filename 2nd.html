About The Magazine

Premier Authority

Ceramic Bangladesh is a bi-monthly magazine focused on Architecture, Real Estate, Construction and Lifestyle in Bangladesh. It also covers the ceramic industry, related sectors, real estate, interior design, culture, innovation, banking and economic issues. In a country with a GDP of $460 billion and a population of 180 million, some 60 per cent of annual investments go to the construction and housing sector, making it the largest sector in the domestic economy of Bangladesh. Published by the Bangladesh Ceramic Manufacturers and Exporters Association (BCMEA), this magazine has been in circulation since 2019, available in both print and digital versions. Led by Chief Editor Mr. Irfan Uddin and renowned journalist Mr. Salahuddin Ahmaad Talukder, the magazine boasts a talented team of writers, reporters, copy editors, designers, and photographers.
